# Pel Dungi

A tiny Mac app. Unnati shows up on your screen with her voice, in three ways:

- **I need motivation**: she floats up on a balloon with a motivation note.
- **Mujhe daanto**: she slides in from the side and scolds you, full voice note.
- **Meeting reminder**: she drives in by car, with a Join meeting button if you add the link.

Pick the first two from the top bar any time, or set any of the three as a reminder.

## Install

Open **Terminal**, paste this line and press Enter:

```
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/teenytinybot/pel-dungi/main/install.sh | bash
```

Then look for Unnati's face in your top bar (near Wi-Fi). Click it to see the options.

## What it does and doesn't do

- Shows a pop-up and plays her voice when you ask or when your reminder is due. That's all.
- It does not read your files, your screen or your messages, and it sends nothing anywhere.
- It opens at login. To stop it, click her face in the top bar and choose Quit.

## Uninstall

Quit it from the top bar, then delete **Pel Dungi** from the Applications folder in your home folder.

Needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer. Works on Apple-chip and Intel Macs.
